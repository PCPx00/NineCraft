# 💜 Contributing to Nine Craft

Thanks for helping make Nine Craft better!

## 🐛 Reporting a bug

Use the **Bug report** template on the [Issues](../../issues/new/choose) page and include:

- your **Nine Craft version** (shown in the CurseForge app, e.g. `v2.0.2`)
- whether it happens in **single player** or on a **server**
- your **allocated RAM**
- what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead
- for crashes: the **crash report** (`crash-reports/` in your instance folder) or `logs/latest.log`, uploaded to [mclo.gs](https://mclo.gs) or a GitHub Gist

## 📖 Quest book problems

If a quest asks for the wrong or a missing item, use the **Quest book issue** template. Say which **chapter** and **quest title** it is. A screenshot helps a lot.

## 💡 Suggestions

Mod suggestions and chapter ideas are welcome. Use the **Suggestion** template and explain how it fits one of the six playstyles: Tech, Magic, RPG, Cozy, Food or Power.

## ✏️ Pull requests

Quest text fixes and translations are the easiest place to start. Quest text lives in `overrides/config/ftbquests/quests/lang/`. Keep pull requests small and describe what you changed.
