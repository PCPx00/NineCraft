## 💜 v2.5.0

**Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1.250 · 512 mods · 43 quest chapters · ~1,650 quests**

### ☠️ The Grim Industries family is complete
Nine Craft now ships all three of xTri6's own mods, and each one has its own quest chapter:

- 🆕 **[Grim Pipe Industries](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/grim-pipe-industries)**: item, fluid and omni pipes, the Reaper Wrench, Soul Links, tag filters and Whispering / Wraithbound upgrades
- 🆕 **[Grim Storage Industries](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/grim-storage-industries)**: a soul-powered storage network with Soul Cable, the Soul Terminal, Stock Keeper, Void Filters, autocrafting and cells all the way up to the Singularity Cell
- ☠️ **[Grim Soul Industries](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/grim-soul-industries)**: updated, and its chapter has been rewritten to match the latest version

### 📖 Quest book: ~1,540 ➜ ~1,650 quests, 41 ➜ 43 chapters
- 🆕 **Grim Pipe Industries chapter** (Storage & Logistics): from the wrench and your first pipes up to Soulforged Omni lines
- 🆕 **Grim Storage Industries chapter** (Storage & Logistics): Soulite ➜ First Network ➜ In and Out ➜ More Than Items ➜ Autocrafting ➜ Infused Soulite ➜ Echo Soulite ➜ Singularity
- ☠️ **Grim Soul Industries chapter rewritten** around the mod's own stages: Reaping ➜ Spawning and Power ➜ Virtualization ➜ Soul Forge. It adds the Harvest, Beheading, No AI, Settings and Boss Core Cards, Soul Steel and the Soul Forge, and drops cards that no longer exist in the mod
- 🪜 **Every chapter now has tier rows:** quests are stacked in labeled rows from top to bottom, for example Inferium ➜ Prudentium ➜ Tertium ➜ Imperium ➜ Supremium ➜ Insanium in Mystical Agriculture, with the chapter's final milestone on the right
- ➡️ **Much cleaner arrows:** arrows stay inside their row, which cuts crossed lines across the whole book from 37 to 3
- 🔗 **New links:** optional quests in *Items on the Move* and *Chests & Drawers* point you to the new Grim chapters, and both new chapters have their own helper rewards and loot-table entries
- ✅ **Checked against the real mods:** every item, mob and dimension the book uses (1,581 of them) matches what's in the game

> ℹ️ Tier rows change the order a little. When a quest needed something from an earlier tier, that requirement now sits on the first quest of its row, so you may need to finish a bit more of the earlier tier before the next row opens.

- [Deeper and Darker](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/deeperdarker): a new dimension beyond the Deep Dark

- [NightLib](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/nightlib) (library)

### 🔄 Updated
- **95 mods updated** to newer versions
- The pack now ships its full **config**, **KubeJS** and **resource pack** setup, including the `9MMCraft_QuestArt` pack used by the quest book's title and tier art
- Recommended RAM is now **about 9.5 GB** (10 GB is a comfortable setting)

> ⚠️ **Updating an existing world?** Back up your world first. Deeper and Darker adds new world generation, which only appears in newly generated chunks. Quest progress carries over, but because the layout changed, a few quests may need re-checking in the book.
